OUR EXPERTISE

Our Core Services

Comprehensive 360º global sourcing, quality assurance, and supply chain solutions.

01

Consultancy Service & Product Development

We provide strategic business advice, sourcing roadmaps, and supplier portfolio optimization across 50+ factories in India. Through our dedicated Design & Development team, we also provide complete end-to-end support to move your chosen product concepts from initial design sketches and material sampling into full container-scale production.

Deliverables: Strategic Sourcing Roadmap Design & Full Development Supplier Portfolio Optimization Cost-Benefit Restructuring
02

Business Advisory & Compliance Audits

Our advisory practice delivers financial forecasting, risk management, and operational efficiency for international buyers. Under Compliance Audits, we promote global standards for social accountability and environmental practices in India—working alongside the Government of India and its member bodies to help exporters achieve certification in Global Best Practices (GBP).

Deliverables: Global Best Practices (GBP) Social & Environmental Audits Financial & Margin Forecasting Supply Chain Risk Mitigation
03

Export & Import Services & Merchandising

Our merchandisers source products to your exact specifications. Every project is assigned a dedicated two-member team to maintain continuous communication and follow-ups between buyers and factories. Combined with proactive Order Management, we provide regular updates throughout the entire production process along with customs clearance and trade compliance.

Deliverables: Dedicated 2-Member Merchandising Team Live Order Management Updates Customs & Export Documentation
04

Market Research & Analytics

Deep-dive target market analysis, competitor price benchmarking, and global consumer trend forecasting. Operating across 4 regional offices in key Indian production cities, our specialists map raw material availability, artisanal craft clusters, and factory capabilities to keep your seasonal collections ahead of market demand.

Deliverables: Competitor Price Benchmarking Artisanal & Material Cluster Mapping Seasonal Consumer Trend Reports
05

Quality Assurance (3-Stage Inspections)

Our QA team averages over 10 years of trade experience. We establish pre-production checklists with factory supervisory teams and conduct a minimum of three inspection stages (Pre-Production, Inline, and Pre-Shipment) for all shipments. Our expertise includes production planning, forecasting, vendor evaluation, and social compliance and packaging audits.

Deliverables: Minimum 3 Inspection Stages Pre-Production Checklists Vendor Evaluation & Packaging Audits
06

Logistics Management (Door-to-Door)

We handle consolidation planning, export documentation, and real-time cargo tracking through to final delivery. Backed by a production capacity exceeding 100+ containers per month across 50+ factories, we operate as a vertically integrated company managing the complete process from factory shipping to final door delivery.

Deliverables: Multi-Cluster Consolidation Planning Documentation & Cargo Tracking Vertically Integrated Door Delivery
